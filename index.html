01 · The floor

Four zones.
One roof.

Walk in and the floor tells you what it is for. Heavy iron on one side, machines that keep your heart honest on the other, and open space in the middle for the work that needs room.

Strength

Racks, barbells and plates for the big lifts — squat, bench, deadlift — with room to load the bar properly.

Cardio

A full treadmill row for warm-ups, steady miles and the days you just need to move.

Functional

Dumbbells, benches and open floor for the work that keeps shoulders, knees and backs in the game.

Racks & plates

Proper racks, proper plates — and enough of them that you never queue for the kit you came for.

02 · Training

Pick a goal.
We’ll build the week.

Four ways to train at Souvenir House of Fitness. Every programme runs on the same floor, with the same coaches watching your form.

Build strength

Progressive barbell work built around the squat, the press and the pull. You add weight in small, planned steps and the coaches check every rep that matters.

  • Barbell-first sessions on the racks
  • Spotted heavy sets, every time
  • For lifters who want numbers that move

Lose fat

Cardio that earns its keep and strength work that keeps the muscle while the weight comes off. Steady sessions, honest effort, no crash diets and no gimmicks.

  • Treadmill and conditioning blocks
  • Strength sessions to hold muscle
  • For anyone who wants to feel lighter on their feet

Build muscle

Dumbbell and machine work, set after set, with the volume and the rest periods that actually grow muscle. The floor has the iron — you bring the consistency.

  • Dumbbell and machine circuits
  • Split routines that hit every group
  • For members chasing size, shape and definition

Start here

Never trained before? Start with a coach beside you. Learn the movements on light weights, find your pace, and build the habit before you chase the numbers.

  • Coached first sessions, light weights
  • Form first, weight second
  • For complete beginners and returners

08 · Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Beginners start with the Start Here programme — a coach trains beside you on light weights until the movements feel natural.

What should I bring for my first session?

Comfortable training clothes, proper training shoes, a water bottle and a towel. Everything else is on the floor.

How much is membership?

Prices on request — call 051 5731690 and the gym will quote you for monthly, quarterly and annual membership on the phone.

What are the opening hours?

Opening times are confirmed by the gym when you call — they change with the season, so the phone has the current answer.

Can I just walk in and look around?

Yes. Come to Plaza 145, Civic Center, and the team will show you the floor and talk through the programmes.

Is there coaching, or do I train alone?

Coaches are on the floor. Heavy sets are spotted, beginners are taught the movements, and anyone can ask for help mid-session.
